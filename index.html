User Guide

Getting Started

This is a complete project management tool that runs entirely in your browser. All data is saved locally.

  • Creating a Project: From the main screen, click "+ Create New Project". Give it a name, choose a type (Tasks or Reserve Study), and select a template to get started quickly.
  • Opening a Project: Simply click on any project card from the main screen to open it.

The Board View

The board is your main workspace, organized into columns that represent stages of your workflow.

  • Columns: You can add, rename, and delete columns. Click and drag a column header to reorder it.
  • Cards: Each card represents a task. Click the global "+" button or the "Add Item" button at the bottom of a column to create a new card. Click on any card to view and edit its details.
  • Drag & Drop: You can drag cards between columns to update their status, or within a column to change their order.

Task Details

Clicking a card opens the detail modal, where you can manage every aspect of the task.

  • Assignees: Assign one or more team members to a task. You can manage your project's team from the Settings menu.
  • Tags: Add colored labels to categorize your tasks. Manage all project tags from the Settings menu.
  • Checklist: Break down a task into smaller sub-items. You can now add due dates to each checklist item.
  • Dependencies: Create relationships between tasks. A task cannot be moved to "Done" if its prerequisite tasks are not complete.
  • Time Tracking: Start and stop a timer on any task to log your work.
  • Recurring Tasks: Set tasks to automatically recreate themselves daily, weekly, or monthly once completed.

Advanced Features

  • Sub-Boards: Convert any task into its own, separate sub-project. This is great for large, complex tasks that need their own board.
  • Automations: Create "if-this-then-that" rules to automate your workflow. For example, automatically assign a user when a card is moved to the "In Progress" column.
  • Gantt & Calendar Views: Visualize your project timeline with a Gantt chart or see your due dates on a monthly calendar.
  • Dashboard: Get a high-level overview of your project's progress.
  • Import/Export: Save your entire project to a JSON file for backup or to share with others.
